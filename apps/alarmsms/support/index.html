Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? Your alarm profiles, phone numbers, SMS commands, icons, colors, and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Does the app send SMS automatically? No. AlarmSMS Controller prepares the SMS command, but you remain in control before sending it.
  • Can I use it with different alarm brands? Yes. The app is designed to be generic, allowing you to create custom SMS commands for different GSM alarm systems.
  • How do backups work? You choose where to save backups using the iOS share sheet or available storage providers.
  • How do I restore purchases? Open AlarmSMS Controller and tap “Restore purchases”.
