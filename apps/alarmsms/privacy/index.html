Privacy Policy

AlarmSMS Controller respects your privacy and is designed to work without collecting personal information, except where required to provide specific app features.

AlarmSMS Controller stores alarm profiles, phone numbers, custom SMS commands, command names, command icons, command colors, app settings, usage limits, and other app-related configuration data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to AlarmSMS Controller servers.

If you choose to use backup, export, or restore features, you decide where your data is saved (for example, iCloud Drive or other storage providers available through the iOS share sheet). AlarmSMS Controller does not have access to these files once exported.

AlarmSMS Controller does not require account registration or user login.

If the app includes subscription or premium features, purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. AlarmSMS Controller may use RevenueCat solely to manage subscription status and premium entitlements within the app. This process may involve the use of an anonymous app-specific identifier and purchase history for the purpose of enabling paid features. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

AlarmSMS Controller does not use advertising services and does not display ads.

AlarmSMS Controller may request access to features such as SMS, phone, contacts, notifications, or local storage only when needed for app functionality. These features may be used for actions such as sending SMS commands to compatible GSM alarm systems, selecting a contact number, showing reminders, or saving and restoring app data. These permissions can be changed or disabled at any time in your device settings.

AlarmSMS Controller does not read incoming SMS messages, does not monitor your communications, and does not send SMS commands automatically without your action.

AlarmSMS Controller does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties.
