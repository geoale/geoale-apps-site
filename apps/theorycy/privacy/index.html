Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

TheoryCY respects your privacy. The app is designed to help users study Cyprus road signs, driving theory, practice questions, mock tests, and related learning material without requiring unnecessary personal information.

Information stored on your device

TheoryCY stores learning progress, completed lessons, practice and mock-test results, preferences, language settings, bookmarks, and other app-related settings locally on your device so that the app can provide its learning features and remember your progress.

Accounts

TheoryCY does not require account registration or a TheoryCY user login.

In-app purchases

TheoryCY offers optional in-app purchases, including 30 Days Pro and Lifetime Pro. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. TheoryCY does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

TheoryCY uses RevenueCat to help verify purchases and manage access to paid features. RevenueCat may process an app-specific identifier, product information, purchase or transaction information, purchase history, and entitlement status needed to provide and restore purchase functionality.

This purchase information is used to provide paid features and restore eligible purchases. TheoryCY does not use this information for third-party advertising tracking.

Analytics and advertising

TheoryCY does not sell your personal information and does not use third-party targeted advertising. TheoryCY does not currently use advertising or cross-app tracking technologies.

If analytics or other data-processing services are introduced in a future version of TheoryCY, this Privacy Policy and the applicable App Store or Google Play privacy disclosures will be updated as required.

Device permissions

TheoryCY may request device permissions only when they are required for a specific feature. You can manage or disable app permissions through your device settings. Core study content does not require access to your contacts, SMS messages, phone calls, or precise location.

Third-party services

TheoryCY uses platform and third-party services where necessary to provide app functionality, including the Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat for purchase-related functionality. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

Children's privacy

TheoryCY is an educational app and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in error, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TheoryCY changes or when legal or platform requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: support.theorycy@geoale.com
