Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

TheoryCY respects your privacy. The app is designed to help users study road signs, driving theory, practice questions, and related learning material for Cyprus without requiring unnecessary personal information.

Information stored on your device

TheoryCY may store learning progress, completed lessons, practice and mock-test results, preferences, language settings, bookmarks, and other app-related settings locally on your device so that the app can provide its learning features and remember your progress.

Accounts

TheoryCY does not require account registration or a user login unless this is clearly introduced as an optional feature in a future version of the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

If TheoryCY offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed by the relevant app store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. TheoryCY does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

TheoryCY may use a third-party purchase-management service, such as RevenueCat, solely to verify purchases and manage access to paid features. Such services may process an anonymous or app-specific identifier and purchase information needed to determine entitlement status. This information is not used by TheoryCY to identify you personally or for advertising tracking.

Analytics and advertising

TheoryCY does not sell your personal information. The app does not use your personal information for third-party targeted advertising. If privacy-respecting analytics are introduced to improve app reliability or learning features, the applicable App Store or Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Device permissions

TheoryCY may request device permissions only when they are required for a specific feature. You can manage or disable app permissions at any time through your device settings. Core study content does not require access to your contacts, SMS messages, phone calls, or precise location.

Third-party services

Some app functionality may rely on platform or third-party services, such as app-store purchase processing. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

Children's privacy

TheoryCY is an educational app and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in error, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TheoryCY changes or when legal or platform requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: support.theorycy@geoale.com
