TheoryCY Contact

If you need help with TheoryCY, find an issue in the learning material, or have a suggestion for the app, please contact us.

Contact

Email: support.theorycy@geoale.com

When reporting a problem

Please include a short description of the issue and, when useful, your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a screenshot. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Common questions

  • Is TheoryCY an official Cyprus government app? No. TheoryCY is an independent educational study app.
  • Does TheoryCY guarantee that I will pass the official theory test? No. It is a study aid and should be used together with current official guidance and suitable driving instruction.
  • Where is my learning progress stored? Learning progress and preferences are generally stored locally on your device unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.
  • I found content that may be incorrect or outdated. What should I do? Please email us with the relevant lesson, sign, or question so it can be reviewed.
  • How do I restore a purchase? If paid features are available, use the Restore Purchases option in TheoryCY or the purchase-management options provided by your app store.
